# 2026 General Election Justice Research

https://exgladous.github.io/2026-general-election-research/

A searchable guide to selected California appellate decisions. Currently scoped to the 13 justices on Marin County's 2026 judicial retention ballot. This project was architected by a human but all searching and summarization was done by either GPT-6 Astra or GPT 6.1 Sol. Attention was paid to have some traceability but the information is provided as is and is used at your own risk.

Cases were discovered through news searches and matched to official court records or the court-designated California Official Reports service on Lexis. News coverage provides discovery leads; court documents support the summaries. GPT-6.1 Sol generated the summaries, and GPT-6 Astra handled searching, source matching, the viewer, and the rest of the project. The viewer's **Review notes** explain the process and limitations.

This is a selected, news-led collection, not a complete or representative record of any justice's work. Summaries and inferred practical significance may contain errors; consult the linked opinions. The project is not a comprehensive current-law check or a voting recommendation.
